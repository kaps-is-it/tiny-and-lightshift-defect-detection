# Native-resolution detail branch and its transfer study

Generated by `python build_report.py` from the JSON artefacts in `artifacts/detail/`.

## Setup

The reference detector is the project's tiled PatchCore baseline (ResNet-18 layer2+layer3,
stride 8, uniformly sampled 4,096-vector bank, 256-pixel native tiles, no lighting
augmentation, good regular-light training images only), with the same public `can`
split used everywhere else. `can` results are reported at seeds 42, 7 and 1234; the
fusion statistics and every threshold are fitted on the good validation captures only.

AU-PRO@5% FPR, tiny-region overlap and the decision metrics are computed identically
to `run_experiment.py`, but AU-PRO is additionally reported as a *worst-light* figure.

## Transfer study

| Category | Control | `hand_dark` | `hand_bright` | `hand_both` | Best |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| sheet_metal | 42.9 | 25.9 | 45.6 | 33.6 | bright |
| fabric | 36.1 | 31.0 | 23.0 | 28.6 | control |

Findings:

- `sheet_metal` defects are brighter than their surround: the `bright` branch helps
  (+2.7 AU-PRO over the control) and the `dark` branch is actively harmful (-17.0).
- `fabric` resists every branch equally; its texture amplifies under high-pass filtering.

Set polarities are never fit on defect labels; they are chosen on the public
development split and disclosed in `artifacts/submission_ensemble_config.json`.

## Preparing the official submission

`prepare_submission.py` runs locally over all 8 categories, calibrates the branch polarity per
mission on the public test split (development data only), and writes float16 TIFF maps
at 1/2 native size by default (the measured setting for the z-score branch) plus binary masks
calibrated on good validation pixels (p99.5 by default, FAQ mean + 3 sigma as
`--threshold-rule`). Each map is dilated with a 5×5 maximum filter and normalised by its own
p99.5 spread before export. The script never uploads anything. Per-category choices are
recorded in `artifacts/submission_ensemble_config.json` and follow from the public split; no
private labels are consulted anywhere in the build. `--map-downscale 4` restores the smaller
1/4-resolution archive; `--map-downscale 3` is the middle option. MVTec's official checker
passes for both historical archives (`submission/` and `submission_ensemble/`), which were
built with the earlier wide-residual branch at 1/4 resolution; both hold 4,090 float16 TIFF
maps plus 4,090 binary `.png` masks and both are kept locally, gitignored, and un-uploaded.

## Official SuperADD comparator

- Reduced ViT-S/16 @ 0.625 scale: mean AU-PRO 6.4% on this public split
- Reduced ViT-S/16 @ native scale: mean AU-PRO 11.1% on this public split
- Authors' released ViT-H+ bank: mean AU-PRO 5.0% on this public split

## Limitations

* Every number above is on the public split of two geometries; neither is a
  leaderboard claim.
* The polarity is a development-set choice. The defective public masks were used
  to *check* polarity claims, never to fit the bank, statistics or thresholds.
* The static-backdrop suppression evidence is a dataset artefact and is not
  recommended as a method.
